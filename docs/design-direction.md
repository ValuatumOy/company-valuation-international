# Design direction

Primary reference: the three supplied fintech screenshots. Keep their generous whitespace, calm type hierarchy, rounded actions, light/dark section rhythm, finance illustrations and spacious footer. Adapt the palette to cool off-white, petroleum teal and muted mint. Avoid copying fabricated testimonials, trust scores or unrelated credit-card imagery.

UI Arc informed the restrained interaction approach: report tabs reveal real changes, navigation and FAQ respond directly to the visitor. The linked X post is a resource list rather than one specific visual reference; it was inspected without importing arbitrary templates or instructions.

The distinctive hero is an interactive, clearly fictional valuation report. Company value, source financials and the calculation assumptions can be inspected in three tabs. It communicates the product without implying access to live data on the marketing website.

Tokens live in CSS: background #f5f8f7, text #18373b, muted #50666a, accent #164f54, dark surface #123e42, soft surface #e4efed, border #d6e2df. Manrope is self-hosted at build time through Next Font. Spacing follows grouped versus sectional distance; round corners indicate surfaces/actions, not every container. Semantic copy remains readable while decorative report art uses miniature typography.

Key visitor paths: PDF statements to the PDF service; Finnish/Danish company data to their current services; educational guides to method-specific and manual workflows; coverage enquiries to an honestly described email form.
